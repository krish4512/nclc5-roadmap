Billing questions

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Cancel from the subscription management page and you keep Pro until the end of the period you've paid for. You won't be charged again.

Do you offer refunds?

If you're not satisfied, email us within 14 days of your first payment and we'll refund it. Renewals aren't refunded, but you can cancel before the next one. The full policy is in the Terms.

Which currency are prices in?

Prices are shown in CAD. Your bank may convert the charge if your card is in another currency.

Is my payment information safe?

Payments are handled entirely by Stripe. Your card details go straight to Stripe and never reach our servers.

Does Pro guarantee I'll pass?

No product can. Pro gives you realistic, timed practice and honest feedback; results depend on the study you put in. Mock-exam scores are indicative, not official TCF or TEF results.
