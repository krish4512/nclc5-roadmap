Questions

Before you start.

Is this for the TCF Canada or the TEF Canada?

Both. The grammar and vocabulary needed for NCLC 5 are the same; where the two exams differ — scoring scales, number of tasks, timing — the roadmap shows each one side by side.

What level do I need to start at?

The course starts at the sound system and the present tense, so a motivated beginner can follow it. It's most efficient if you're around A1–A2 and need a structured path to B1.

Does it guarantee a score?

No. It's an independent study resource, not affiliated with the organisations that run the TCF or TEF, and mock-exam results are indicative only. What it gives you is a clear plan and honest feedback on where you are.

Where is my progress stored?

In your browser, on your device. Nothing you tick or type is sent to us. You can export a backup file from the roadmap page and import it on another device.
