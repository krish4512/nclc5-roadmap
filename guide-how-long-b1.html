How long does it take to reach B1?

Realistic time estimates to reach NCLC 5, depending on where you start and how much you study.

Updated October 2, 2026

NCLC 5 is roughly the same as level B1 on the European scale (CEFR). How long it takes depends mostly on two things: where you start and how many hours a day you study.

Our estimates

Where you start1 hour a day2 hours a day
True beginner12–15 months6–8 months
Around A2 (simple conversations)5–7 months3–4 months
Around B1, needs exam technique6–10 weeks4–6 weeks

These are estimates for steady, focused study, not promises. Everyone's pace is different. Daily study beats long weekend sessions.

What makes it faster

  • Short daily reviews. Spacing your reviews over days helps you remember far more than cramming. Our daily review does the spacing for you.
  • Testing yourself. Trying to recall an answer teaches you more than re-reading it. That's why every lesson ends with a drill.
  • Speaking out loud from week one. Use listen and repeat and record yourself.
  • Learning the exam format early. Templates for Task 3 and Task 2 save weeks at the end.

Booking your test date

Book when your mock exam shows NCLC 5 or higher in all four skills, ideally twice in a row. If your test date is close and you're still a beginner, it's usually better to move the date than to sit the test too early.
