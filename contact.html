Studying

Where should I start?

Open the roadmap and work through the grammar stages in order, alongside one vocabulary bank at a time. Drill each topic in Practice until you score above 90%, then test yourself with a mock exam.

TCF Canada or TEF Canada — which should I take?

Both are accepted by IRCC for Express Entry and other programmes. The TCF writing and speaking sections are shorter; the TEF plays each listening recording only once. Many people choose based on test-date availability near them. The roadmap shows both formats side by side.

How accurate are the mock-exam bands?

They're a rough guide from short samples. Use them to track your trend over time rather than as a prediction of your official score.

Audio, voice and translation

The French audio sounds English, or doesn't play.

Audio uses the voices installed on your device. Add a French voice in your system settings (on Windows: Settings → Time & language → Speech; on macOS: System Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content; on Android and iOS: text-to-speech settings), then reload. On the roadmap page you can pick a specific voice.

The microphone button doesn't appear.

Dictation depends on your browser's speech recognition, which Chrome, Edge and Safari provide and Firefox currently doesn't. Speaking-task recording works in all modern browsers once you allow microphone access.

Can I trust the translator?

It's machine translation — great for checking a word or a phrase, not for official documents. When it falls back to a translation memory, it labels the result as approximate.

Progress and account

Do I need an account?

No. Your progress is saved in your browser. If you subscribe to Pro, Stripe keeps your billing details so you can manage the subscription.

My progress disappeared.

Progress lives in your browser's storage, so it's lost if you clear site data, use private browsing, or switch device or browser. Use Export progress regularly to keep a backup, and Import to restore it.
