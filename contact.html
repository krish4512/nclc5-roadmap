Studying

Where should I start?

Take the 2-minute level check, then work through the course in order from the module it suggests. Do your Daily review each day, then test yourself with a mock exam.

TCF Canada or TEF Canada — which should I take?

Both are accepted by IRCC for Express Entry and other programmes. The TCF writing and speaking sections are shorter; the TEF plays each listening recording only once. Many people choose based on test-date availability near them. Our TCF vs TEF guide compares both formats.

How accurate are the mock-exam bands?

They're a rough guide from short samples. Use them to track your trend over time rather than as a prediction of your official score.

Audio, voice and translation

The French audio sounds English, or doesn't play.

Audio uses the voices installed on your device. Add a French voice in your system settings (on Windows: Settings → Time & language → Speech; on macOS: System Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content; on Android and iOS: text-to-speech settings), then reload. You can pick a specific voice and a slower speed under the Aa button.

Recording doesn't work.

Speaking-task recording works in all modern browsers once you allow microphone access. If you blocked it, allow the microphone for this site in your browser settings, then reload.

Progress and account

Do I need an account?

No. Your progress is saved in your browser. If you subscribe to Pro, Stripe keeps your billing details so you can manage the subscription.

My progress disappeared.

Progress lives in your browser's storage, so it's lost if you clear site data, use private browsing, or switch device or browser. Use Export progress regularly to keep a backup, and Import to restore it.
