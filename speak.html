Listen & repeat

Copying a model voice — rhythm, linking and melody, not just the words — is one of the fastest ways to sound natural. Record yourself and compare.

  1. 1 · ListenPlay the sentence. Slow it down if you need to.
  2. 2 · RecordSay it straight away, copying the melody.
  3. 3 · CompareHear the model, then you. Spot one difference, try again.

    Recordings stay in this browser tab — nothing is uploaded — and disappear when you leave the page.
