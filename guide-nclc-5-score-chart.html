NCLC 5 score chart

The exact TCF Canada and TEF Canada scores you need for NCLC 5, skill by skill.

Updated October 2, 2026

To get NCLC 5 in French, you need NCLC 5 in each of the four skills — listening, reading, writing and speaking. A high score in one skill does not make up for a low score in another. Here are the scores that count for each level on the two French tests Canada accepts: TCF Canada and TEF Canada.

Listening and reading are scored out of 699. Writing and speaking are scored out of 20.

How to read your result

  • Each skill counts separately. Programs check each skill on its own, so plan to reach the target in all four.
  • Writing and speaking are the usual blockers. On the TCF, NCLC 5 is a single point (6/20). A small gain in writing or speaking can move you a full level.
  • Results are valid for two years from the test date for Express Entry.

Check the official chart

Score tables can change. Before you book or submit, check the current equivalency chart on the Government of Canada website: Language test equivalency charts (canada.ca).
