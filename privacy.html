2. What stays on your device

The Service works without an account. Everything you do while studying is saved only in your browser's local storage, on your own device, and is never sent to us:

  • ticked checklist items, completed modules, the module you were last on, and the days you studied (for your streak);
  • practice and mock-exam scores, and which drill items you got right or wrong;
  • writing-task drafts, your display preferences (theme, text size, font, voice, playback speed, drill settings), your exam date, and the name you type on your certificate;
  • speaking-task recordings, which are kept in memory for playback and are discarded when you leave the page unless you choose to download them.

You can erase this data at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser settings. The Export and Import buttons on the roadmap page move it between devices as a file that you control.

3. What we collect

When you subscribe

Payments are processed by Stripe. To create and manage your subscription, Stripe collects your name, email address, billing address and payment card details. We receive your name, email address, country, subscription status and history, and limited card information (such as card brand and last four digits). We never see or store your full card number.

When you contact us

If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to include in your message.

When you report a mistake

If you use a "Report a mistake" button, we receive your message, the page and lesson it refers to, and your email address only if you choose to give it. We use this to correct the material and, if you asked, to reply to you.

When you sign up for the free cheat sheets

If the cheat-sheet page asks for your email address, we receive that address and the date you signed up. We use it to send you the cheat sheets and occasional study tips and product news. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and you can ask us to delete your address at any time.

Technical information

Like any website, the servers that deliver these pages — operated by our hosting provider — automatically receive standard request information such as your IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time of the request. This is used to deliver the site and keep it secure. We do not use analytics or advertising trackers.

5. How we use information

  • to provide your subscription, process payments, and send receipts and service messages;
  • to respond to your questions and support requests;
  • to send the free cheat sheets and study emails you signed up for, until you unsubscribe;
  • to prevent fraud and meet our legal, tax and accounting obligations.

We do not sell or rent personal information, and we do not use it for advertising. Where the GDPR applies, we rely on performance of our contract with you, our legal obligations, your consent (for study emails, which you can withdraw at any time), and our legitimate interest in running and securing the Service.

6. Cookies

We do not set advertising or analytics cookies. The Service uses your browser's local storage for the features described in section 2. Stripe sets its own cookies on its checkout and billing pages to process payments and prevent fraud.

7. How long we keep it

We keep subscription and payment records for as long as your subscription is active and afterwards for as long as tax and accounting laws require (in Canada, generally six years). Support emails are kept for as long as needed to help you and then deleted. Data in your browser stays there until you clear it.

8. Where it is processed

Our service providers, including Stripe and Google, may process information in Canada, the United States and other countries whose privacy laws may differ from yours. Where required, they use contractual safeguards to protect it.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live — including under Canada's PIPEDA, Quebec's Law 25, and the GDPR — you may have the right to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, to withdraw consent, to object to or restrict certain processing, and to receive a copy of your data. Email us to make a request; we will reply within 30 days. You may also complain to your privacy regulator, such as the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada or the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec.
