Updates

What's new

New features and fixes, newest first. Got an idea or found a mistake? Tell us.

  1. October 6, 2026

    We're now Prêt FrançaisNew

    • A new name for the course: Prêt Français — prêt means “ready”. Same free course, same goal: your French, exam-ready for NCLC 5 on the TCF Canada and TEF Canada.
    • A new logo: a speech bubble — speaking French — carrying the circumflex of prêt, which also reads as a step up. You'll see it on the site, your home-screen icon, link previews, the cheat sheets, your shareable progress card and the certificate.
    • Your progress, streak and Daily review are untouched.
  2. October 5, 2026

    A simpler site: four places, four steps

    • The menu is now just Today · Course · Review · Exam.
    • Every module follows the same four steps: Lessons → Practice → Check → Done. Pronunciation notes and common mistakes now sit inside the lesson they belong to, the writing task is part of the Check, and each module's key words go into your Daily review automatically.
    • Honest times: each module shows how long it really takes (about 15–30 minutes), worked out from its content.
    • Exam prep: speaking topics, writing models and the exam guides on one page.
    • The drills, verb conjugator, listen and repeat and grammar reference pages were folded into the course and Daily review. Old links still work.
    • Back up your progress from the Today page, and choose the French voice and audio speed under the Aa button.
  3. October 4, 2026

    Listen, write and speak inside every module

    • Dictation: three sentences per module — listen (normal or slow), type, and see every missing word and accent.
    • Write it: a short exam-style writing task in 19 modules, with a checklist that ticks as you type, “watch out” hints for classic mistakes, a model answer, and your draft saved.
    • Say it: say a line and your browser checks which words it understood (Chrome, Edge, Safari).
    • Listen first: hide the Real life text and answer from the audio alone, like the TCF listening test.
    • Each module now shows how much you've done, and marks itself complete when every part is finished.
    • The TCF Task 2 and Task 3 kits are now short and focused: just the lessons, examples and a quick check (about an hour each).
    • Modules are simpler: the listening practice (Real life, dictation, Say it) and the shadowing section were removed, so each module is lessons, practice, writing and a check.
  4. October 3, 2026

    Practise inside every lesson

    • A cheat-code picture at the top of every lesson: the rule at a glance, with audio for the French examples.
    • Try it: 2–3 quick questions at the end of every lesson, with instant feedback. Accents are forgiven but pointed out.
    • Anything you get wrong — in a lesson, a module check or a checkpoint — goes straight into your Daily review, with a link back to the lesson.
    • Real life: a short text in every module — a text message, a notice, an email, a voicemail or a conversation — with audio, English and three questions.
    • The free cheat sheets now include a page of visual cheat codes for each module.
    • A1, A2 and B1 checkpoints: 15 mixed questions per level, with a badge when you pass.
  5. October 2, 2026

    Full mock exam, speaking topics and writing models

    • Full mock exam in one sitting: all four skills back to back, then a report with an estimated NCLC level for each skill.
    • Speaking topics: 60 TCF-style prompts for Task 2 and Task 3, with a random topic button, the real timers and recording.
    • Writing models: the same TCF writing task answered at NCLC 4, 5 and 7, with mistakes marked and explained.
    • Free exam guides: NCLC 5 score chart, TCF vs TEF, Task 2 and Task 3 examples, and how long it takes to reach B1.
    • A certificate of completion when you finish all 21 modules.
  6. October 1, 2026

    Simpler navigation and a Today page

    • Four sections only: Today, Learn, Practice and Exam, each with a few clear tabs.
    • Today: continue where you left off, daily review, streak, word and mistake of the day, exam countdown, badges, and a progress card to share.
    • Daily review with spaced repetition, so words come back just before you forget them.
    • Verb conjugator for 360+ verbs, with sound (since folded into the course).
    • Listen and repeat: record yourself and compare with the model voice (since folded into the course).
    • Reading settings (the Aa button): text size, an easy-to-read font, and dark mode.
    • Find your level: a 12-question check that tells you where to start.
    • A “Report a mistake” button on every lesson and drill.
    • Better layout on phones, a home-screen icon and link previews when you share the site.
  7. September 29, 2026

    Free cheat sheets

    • One-page PDF cheat sheets for every module, plus one file with all of them.
  8. September 27, 2026

    The full course

    • A complete course from beginner to B1 in 21 modules, with English translations.
    • TCF speaking kits for Task 2 and Task 3, with answer builders.
    • A new design, colour-coded levels, drills and a timed mock exam.
